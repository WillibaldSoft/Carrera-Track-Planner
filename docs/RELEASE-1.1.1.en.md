# Version 1.1.1 – Diodes left of the slot

Start/CU sensors, fuel sensor, stripes and FUEL lettering sit left of the slot in driving direction. Active straight, double, curved and pit-entry switches show receiving diodes on the left. Passive exits and second modules gain no extra diode. Artwork follows rail rotation and is shared by 2D, 3D and vector export. Rail geometry, heights, saved label positions and AKS metadata are retained.
