# Version 1.1.6 – Independent layer checkboxes

The central **Layers** button opens one checkbox per layer. Any combination can remain visible, such as layers 0 and 2 while hiding layer 1. All shown rails remain selectable and editable together.

- **Show all** restores the complete view. **Hide all** provides guidance and prevents invisible new-rail placement.
- **Only this** isolates one layer in a single click.
- With several layers checked, **New rails on layer** chooses the destination of new rails. With a restricted visibility selection, selecting a rail adopts its layer as the default.
- Selections on layers that remain visible are retained. Hidden selections are removed; an assembly with hidden members is deselected as a whole. Assemblies become jointly editable when all their layers are visible.
- Checkboxes, shortcuts, insertion choice and help stay together. The panel stays open during checkbox changes, closes with Escape or outside click and avoids toolbar clipping on narrow screens.

Stacking order, heights and saved layouts stay unchanged. Shoulders, pit pieces, connection handles and height labels follow layer visibility.
