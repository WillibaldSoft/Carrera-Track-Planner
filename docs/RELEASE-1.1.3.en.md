# Version 1.1.3 – Continue shoulders one rail at a time

Shoulders have a dark drift apron and a narrow red/white outer band. Each piece receives its own contour-following colour segments: straight, inner curve, outer curve and end piece. Straight-track spacing follows the six red segments visible in the official product photo.

In **Automatic**, choose left, right or both sides. Hover and click a rail, or use **Attach shoulders and continue**. The side stays selected and the next physically connected, eligible rail is proposed. Filled or blocked sides are skipped; reversed rails swap sides to preserve driving direction. Continuation stops at open ends or ambiguous junctions. Each placement supports undo. The view remains stable. Newly joined shoulders lose redundant internal end pieces while free ends and opposite shoulders are preserved.

2D, 3D and vector export share the same colour polygons. Track geometry, heights and manual label positions are retained. Printed details are an original vector interpretation of product photos using the existing outlines.
