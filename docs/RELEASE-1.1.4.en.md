# Version 1.1.4 – Shoulders with newly laid rails

Enable **Add shoulders with new rails** in the left catalogue, then choose **Left**, **Right** or **Both sides**. The choice survives catalogue changes and repeated placement. Insertion before/after a selection, catalogue drag-and-drop and the first placement on an empty canvas use it too. The repeat preview includes shoulders; one undo removes rail and shoulders together. Existing rails keep their settings. Blocked sides, including the CU housing, are skipped.

For the pit lane, select an individual pit piece, open **Borders** in the catalogue and attach an offered shoulder. Entrances/exits use shaped 20602 outside shoulders; single/fuel straights use 20560 straight shoulders on either side. Occupied attachments are not offered again. Native plans preserve these parts for inventory, 2D/3D and export. Exactly docked pit shoulders follow coordinate edits, rotation and carrier changes of the individual pit piece. Independently moved shoulders remain separate.
